Incident Response Runbook

How Northwind engineering declares, runs and closes production incidents.

Severity levels

SeverityDefinitionResponse timeWho is paged
SEV1Claims intake or payments down for all customers15 minutes On-call engineer, incident commander and the Head of Engineering
SEV2A major feature down or degraded in one region30 minutes On-call engineer and incident commander
SEV3A minor feature degraded, with a workaround4 business hours On-call engineer
SEV4Cosmetic issue or internal toolingNext business day The owning team's queue

Declaring an incident

  1. Open an incident in PagerDuty against the affected service
    1. Pick the severity from the table above
    2. Name an incident commander; it is never the person fixing the problem
  2. Create a Slack channel named #inc-YYYYMMDD-short-name and invite the responders
  3. Post a status update every 30 minutes for SEV1 and every hour for SEV2

Rolling back a deployment

When an incident starts within 30 minutes of a deploy, roll back first and investigate later.

kubectl rollout undo deployment/claims-api -n claims-prod
kubectl rollout status deployment/claims-api -n claims-prod

After the incident

  • Write a postmortem within 5 business days for SEV1 and SEV2
    • Use the blameless template in Confluence
    • Review it in the Thursday reliability meeting
  • File follow-up tickets in the RELY Jira project
